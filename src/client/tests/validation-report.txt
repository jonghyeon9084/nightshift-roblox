Wire contract:
- Action RemoteEvent: exactly ProtocolVersion, RequestId, Action, StoreId,
  RoundId, SeatGeneration, Payload. JoinStore uses the target StoreId and false
  RoundId. All other IDs/revisions/tokens originate in server state.
- ActionResult RemoteEvent: ProtocolVersion/RequestId identify outstanding input;
  Ok/Code/MoveCode drive feedback, and failure schedules a limited snapshot.
- State RemoteEvent: ProtocolVersion/Kind/Sequence/ServerTime and replacement
  Self/Stores/Store slices. False clears Store. StoreId and monotone Revision
  checks reject stale/cross-store details. No teamID alias is invented.
- GetSnapshot RemoteFunction: {ProtocolVersion}; response Ok/Code/RetryAfter/
  Snapshot. Events subscribe first. One outstanding Invoke, >=2s spacing,
  <=2 automatic attempts, manual retry, explicit failure at 10 seconds.
  A hung Invoke keeps its in-flight lock until its response to avoid concurrent
  calls; State events, movement and the existing HUD continue independently.

Implemented flows:
- Four-store summary and keyboard 1/2/3/4 selection / touch buttons; M navigation.
- Roster and ready state; E prepare/cancel; P explicit solo practice.
- Shelf names/glyphs, all order lines/statuses, carrying, counter put-down and
  acceptance with E; R free return. Orders/carry remain visible during outage.
- Per-role owners, indices, connection/share state, missing segments, private
  ReadShapes and team SharedShapes. E claim/share; Q release; 1/2/3 input.
- G contextual position signal with sender name and marker; 5-second expiry.
- Recovery pause, E resume-ready, F survivor solo finish, 10-second solo request
  and peer cancellation; waiting players see their restricted status.
- Server-classified result/reason/history and E free retry to preparation.
- H help; B free reposition; X leave; F5 state retry; Tab fold. All core controls
  have touch buttons. Timers use server time and the Store slice sample time.
- Optional preparation-only apron-color swatch and local BoxHandleAdornment;
  explicit free/not-for-sale/no-ownership language; no purchase or asset API.
- Marker binding is 5s + 2s delay + one retry; manual retry/reposition/Hub exit.
  Unloaded markers remove labels/connections and report SetLoaded=false.
  Successful binding reports true and requests snapshot. Role renewals are 2s.
